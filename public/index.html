    About

    Choral and sacred music for voices

    Matthew Menard is a Taiwanese-Canadian composer and bass singer from Ottawa. His focus is choral and sacred composition. He is composer-in-residence at St. Joseph’s Parish in downtown Ottawa, and his music has been performed by the Capital Chamber Choir, Ontario Youth Choir, York University Chamber Choir, and the Ottawa Bach Choir.
