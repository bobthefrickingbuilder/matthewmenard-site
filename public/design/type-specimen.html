Matthew Menard

Composer · Bass · Ottawa

Choral and sacred music for voices

Matthew Menard is a Taiwanese-Canadian composer and bass singer, born in Ottawa, Ontario in 2004. Today, his focus is primarily on choral and sacred composition. He serves as composer-in-residence at St. Joseph’s Parish in downtown Ottawa.

His music has been performed by ensembles including the Capital Chamber Choir, Ontario Youth Choir, York University Chamber Choir, and the Ottawa Bach Choir.

Each work is my invitation—to walk the emotional path that gave it life.

How they differ

  • A reads as engraved and classical, close to a printed score title page. The sans body keeps long text and forms clean. Lightest download.
  • B puts a contemporary, slightly quirky display face over a bookish serif. More editorial, less traditional. Twice the font weight to download.
  • Neither face is used on the current site (Cormorant Garamond + Lora) or in the reserved list (Glacial Indifference, Caprasimo, Figtree, Monoglyphic).
