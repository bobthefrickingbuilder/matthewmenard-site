      Request button for client sites

      Put this link in the footer of every page of a client's site (style it like that site's buttons):

      <a href="https://www.matthewmenardmusic.com/alterations" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Request a change</a>

      It opens the request page. The first time on each device, the client types the email you have for them and gets a one-time sign-in link; after that they stay signed in for about four months. Anyone else gets nothing, since their email is not on your list. Pausing a client signs them out everywhere.
