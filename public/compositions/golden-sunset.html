The Golden Sunset

A setting of Samuel Longfellow’s poem on the passage between day and night.

Voicing
Baritone and piano
Text
Samuel Longfellow

About the work

An art song for baritone and piano, setting Samuel Longfellow’s meditation on the dissolving boundary between earth and sky at dusk. The vocal writing moves in extended, expressive phrases aligned to the text’s natural contours, while the piano sustains a consistent atmosphere of quiet radiance throughout.

The text progresses from vivid natural imagery toward reflection on mortality and spiritual transcendence, making it well suited to recital programming alongside Romantic and late-Romantic repertoire.
